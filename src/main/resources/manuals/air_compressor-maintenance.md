# Rotary Screw Air Compressor – Maintenance Manual

## 1. Scope

Covers the oil-injected rotary screw compressor in the compressor house. Nominal values: motor speed 1480 rpm, discharge pressure 7.5 bar, element outlet temperature 70–80 °C, overall vibration below 3.5 mm/s RMS.

## 2. High discharge temperature

High element outlet temperature is the most frequent compressor alarm. The compressor trips automatically at about 105 °C to protect the oil and the screw element. Typical causes, in order of likelihood:

- Blocked or dirty oil cooler or aftercooler fins. Clean with compressed air from the inside out, or with a low-pressure water wash for oily deposits.
- Cooling fan failure or wrong rotation direction.
- Low oil level. Check the sight glass with the unit stopped and top up with the specified compressor oil.
- Thermostatic (thermal bypass) valve stuck closed, so oil bypasses the cooler.
- Ambient temperature in the compressor room above 40 °C because of poor ventilation.

A slow rise over days usually indicates progressive cooler fouling. A fast rise points to fan or valve failure.

## 3. Oil and filter service

Change oil and the oil filter every 4000 hours or yearly. Replace the air intake filter when the differential pressure indicator shows red. Replace the oil separator element when separator pressure drop exceeds 1 bar.

## 4. Low discharge pressure

Falling system pressure with the compressor fully loaded indicates air demand above capacity or leaks in the distribution network. Carry out an ultrasonic leak survey; leaks typically waste 20–30% of compressed air. Also check the inlet valve opens fully and the minimum pressure valve is not sticking.

## 5. Vibration

Rising vibration on the motor or element bearings indicates bearing wear, coupling misalignment or a loose mounting. Element bearing failure is serious and requires an element overhaul by the manufacturer's service team.
