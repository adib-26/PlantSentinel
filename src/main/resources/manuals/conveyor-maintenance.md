# Belt Conveyor Drive – Maintenance Manual

## 1. Scope

Belt conveyor drive units (motor, gearbox, drive pulley) on the packing lines. Nominal values: motor speed 1450 rpm, gearbox temperature 35–45 °C, vibration below 1.8 mm/s RMS, drive torque about 25 N·m, pneumatic belt tensioner pressure 5 bar.

## 2. Belt slip and speed variation

Speed fluctuation with normal torque indicates belt slip on the drive pulley or a problem with the variable-frequency drive or speed encoder. Check belt tension and the pneumatic tensioner pressure, clean the pulley lagging, and check the drive fault log.

## 3. Jams and overload

Rising torque with falling speed indicates a jam, product build-up under the belt, seized idler rollers or misaligned guides. Stop the line, lock out, and remove the obstruction. Rotate idlers by hand; replace any that do not turn freely.

## 4. Tensioner pressure loss

The pneumatic take-up loses pressure through leaking hoses, fittings or a failing regulator. Low tension causes slip and belt wander. Leak-test with soapy water and replace damaged fittings.

## 5. Gearbox overheating and vibration

Check gearbox oil level monthly. Overheating with rising vibration indicates gear or bearing wear; take an oil sample for wear-metal analysis.
