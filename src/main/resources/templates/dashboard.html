How to read the haunting
  • Aura and meter: failure risk. Green is low, amber medium, orange high, red critical, grey not assessed. A stronger glow means a higher probability.
  • Flicker and a peeking ghost: health WARNING or CRITICAL, or risk HIGH or above. Trembling: health or risk CRITICAL.
  • Cobwebs and fading: readings are late, have stopped, or none arrived in the last hour.
  • Ghost beside a count or an alert: anomalies.
  • EMF meter: the needle is the mean failure risk; each light is one machine, coloured by its risk level.
  • Candle line: red when a critical alert came in during the last hour, amber when a machine needs attention.
  • Kiro in the corner: worried when a machine's health or risk is CRITICAL, uneasy when its health is WARNING or its risk HIGH. The badge counts them.
  • On other pages, evidence is stone (measured), etched (rule-based), misty (model estimate), parchment (documented) or ghostly (AI interpretation, not verified). A poltergeist, an apparition, an omen and a grave are a sensor fault, an anomaly, a prediction and a confirmed failure. A wax seal marks a recommendation that a second engineer approved.
