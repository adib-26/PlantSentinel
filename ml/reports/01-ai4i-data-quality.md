Findings: the label is heavily imbalanced (about 1 in 30), so accuracy is misleading and PR-AUC, recall and F1 are the primary metrics. The label and failure-mode flags disagree on a small number of rows (mostly random failures, RNF), which caps achievable recall. The failure-mode flags are excluded from the features because they are the label broken down by cause (target leakage).
